# Finder — booking and customer support

> Reservations, recommendations, and support requests are brought together in one product.

## Business problem

Booking requires current availability, customer request handling, and visibility for venue managers.

## What we built

A restaurant booking platform with real-time table availability, a management analytics dashboard, and an ML-powered chatbot.

**Workflow:** Real-time availability → reservation → customer support → analytics.

## When this experience is relevant

Teams building booking services or digital products with ongoing customer interaction.

## Technical foundation

Python · FastAPI · React · WebSockets.
