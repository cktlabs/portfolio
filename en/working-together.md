# Working with CKT Labs

## 1. Understand the business problem

We discuss your goal, current system, integrations, and constraints. An NDA can be signed before confidential details are shared. We identify unknowns affecting the scope and estimate.

**You receive:** an initial view of fit and what needs to be clarified.

## 2. Agree on scope and a plan

We prepare a technical brief, architecture outline, stack recommendations, and an estimate. Scope is agreed before development; changes are discussed separately.

**You receive:** a delivery plan and a basis for deciding whether to proceed.

## 3. Develop with regular demonstrations

A team with the required disciplines works on the project. You communicate directly with engineers and see progress in weekly demonstrations. Security and integration checks form part of delivery.

**You receive:** the ability to assess progress through working software.

## 4. Launch and hand over

We deploy the system, hand over source code, documentation, and operating instructions, and transfer knowledge to your team. Post-launch support is agreed separately.

**You receive:** a working system and the materials to operate and develop it.
